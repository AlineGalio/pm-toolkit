4. Definitions

  • Velocity: story points of stories that met the Definition of Done in the sprint.
  • Commitment reliability: completed points divided by planned points.
  • Lead time: median calendar days from "ready" (DoR met) to running in production.
  • Bug: defect found after merge. Incident: Sev1 or Sev2 within 48 h of a release.
  • Debt hours closed: hours of assessment items EX-01, 02, 03, 04, 06 and 08 (272 of 860 h).
