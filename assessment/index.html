2. Method

Scoring

Score = Risk × Impact (1–25). Effort in hours is reported separately and does not change the score.

  • Risk (1–5): likelihood that the debt causes an incident, breach or failed release.
  • Impact (1–5): business and operational damage if it happens.
  • Effort: estimate validated with the engineering team, in hours.

Quadrants

  • Act now score ≥ 16
  • Plan score 9–15
  • Defer score 4–8
  • Accept score ≤ 3 (documented, reviewed later)
